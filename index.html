WebAR · Construction Visualization

Structure AR

Visualize construction components in your own space, right from your browser.

How to use

  1. Tap Start AR.
  2. Allow camera access when prompted.
  3. Move your phone slowly to detect a surface.
  4. Select a construction component from the bottom bar.
  5. Tap the detected surface to place it.
  6. Tap a placed component to select it.
  7. Move, rotate, scale, duplicate or delete it.
  8. Add multiple components to build the structure.

No AR-capable device? Use 3D Preview to try the same tools with your mouse or finger, orbiting a virtual scene instead of your camera.
